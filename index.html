How to play

  1. One person guessesSomeone chosen at random calls out the percentage they think answers the question.
  2. Everyone else votesIn the meeting poll, the group votes on whether the real answer is higher or lower than that guess.
  3. The answer is revealedWe see if the group called it, using real industry benchmarks.
  4. We talk it throughAbout five minutes per question on what it means for how we work.
